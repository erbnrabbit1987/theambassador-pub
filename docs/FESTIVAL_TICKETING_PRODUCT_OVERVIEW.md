# Festival Ticketing Platform - Product Overview

## Executive Summary

The Festival Ticketing Platform is a comprehensive multi-event ticketing system designed for low-budget festivals where artist fees are tied directly to ticket sales performance. The platform features a sophisticated Ambassador system that enables dynamic pricing, discount management, and revenue tracking across multiple concurrent festivals.

## Core Value Proposition

1. **Multi-Event Management**: Support for multiple festivals running in parallel with independent pricing, phases, and artist lineups
2. **Artist Break-Even Model**: Transparent tracking of ticket sales toward artist contracted fees, enabling a sustainable low-budget festival model
3. **Dual Ambassador System**: Two distinct ambassador types (Individual and Team) with different pricing and commission structures
4. **Dynamic Pricing**: Time-based ticket phases with flexible discount mechanisms
5. **Guest Ticket System**: Controlled distribution of free tickets through ambassadors

## Key Features

### Public-Facing Features
- Festival discovery and browsing
- Artist lineup and event details
- Multi-phase ticket purchasing with dynamic pricing
- Ambassador code redemption
- Guest ticket redemption
- User account management and ticket history

### Ambassador Features
- Unique code generation (single-use, 24-hour validity)
- Real-time sales dashboard
- Quota management (tickets and guest tickets)
- Commission tracking (for Individual Ambassadors)
- Sales analytics and reporting

### Artist Features
- Break-even progress tracking
- Revenue and ticket sales analytics
- Event-specific performance metrics
- Post break-even profit visibility

### Admin Features
- Complete festival and event management
- Ambassador creation and configuration
- Artist fee and break-even target management
- Global pricing and discount rule configuration
- Comprehensive reporting and analytics
- System-wide settings and permissions

## Business Model

### Low-Budget Festival Philosophy
The platform supports festivals where:
- Artist fees are not guaranteed upfront
- Fees are tied to ticket sales performance
- Artists have contracted break-even targets
- Revenue after break-even contributes to festival sustainability

### Revenue Streams
1. **Ticket Sales**: Primary revenue from ticket purchases
2. **Phase-Based Pricing**: Higher prices in later phases incentivize early purchases
3. **Ambassador Commissions**: Calculated costs for Individual Ambassadors based on tiered structures
4. **Post Break-Even Revenue**: Profit generated after artists reach their targets

## Target Users

1. **Public Visitors**: General ticket buyers seeking festival access
2. **Ambassadors**: Sales representatives (Individual or Team) promoting festivals
3. **Artists/Artist Teams**: Performers with break-even contracts
4. **Festival Organizers/Admins**: Platform administrators managing events, ambassadors, and artists

## Platform Goals

1. **Transparency**: Clear visibility into ticket sales, artist progress, and ambassador performance
2. **Flexibility**: Configurable pricing, discounts, and quotas that adapt to festival needs
3. **Scalability**: Support for multiple concurrent festivals with independent configurations
4. **User Experience**: Intuitive interfaces for all user types
5. **Financial Control**: Comprehensive tracking of revenue, discounts, commissions, and break-even status

## Success Metrics

- Ticket sales volume across phases
- Ambassador code redemption rates
- Artist break-even achievement rates
- Revenue generation and distribution
- User satisfaction and platform adoption
- System performance and reliability
